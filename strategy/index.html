02 · Spectrum

Local growth is not a funnel graphic. It is a chain of moments — from the listing someone finds to the call they make.

Listing→Website→Ads→Reputation→Retained growth

The goal is not maximum spend — it is appropriate presence. A business should know where it shows up, what it promises, and how a stranger becomes a customer.

06 · Notes

Working notes on local digital — as they happen.

Observations from the field. No invented case-study scores — just patterns worth writing down.

MapsSep 2026

The listing is the homepage

For many trades and local services, the first meaningful screen is not the website — it is the Google Business profile. Hours wrong, photos stale, or services blank means the site never gets a fair chance.

OffersAug 2026

Clarity beats clever

Buyers under time pressure scan for who you serve, what you fix, and how to reach you. Ambiguous hero lines and buried phone numbers cost more than a dull but honest page.

AdsJul 2026

Spend without a surface is waste

Paid search into a slow mobile site or a listing that does not match the ad promise trains Google and the customer to distrust you. Fix the landing before you scale the bid.

ReviewsJun 2026

VOC is a copy brief

The phrases customers use in reviews — showed up, explained the options, did not upsell — are often better positioning than anything written in a brand workshop.
